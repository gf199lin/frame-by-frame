## 重新生成

```powershell
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "outputs/demo-video/_work/vo/gen-vo-v3.ps1"
node outputs/demo-video/_work/build/audio-subs-v3.mjs
```

改文案时同步更新 `_work/vo/lines-v3.json`，脚本会按新时长重算字幕；若某句超出所属段落，脚本会打印 WARN。
